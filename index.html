Three paths to adoption

How we help

Develop the people and processes to turn technology into growth.

Support for founders and leaders building and scaling companies.

01

Get technology adopted

Getting technology adopted requires a sale, whether you’re a founder winning customers or an internal champion influencing colleagues. You need to understand and articulate its value, build support among the people who matter, and design pilots that generate credible evidence. We help you navigate that process, turning early interest into commitment and technology embedded in everyday work.

02✦

Design an AI-native organisation

Running and scaling a technology-enabled company means rethinking how work gets done: the outcomes, workflows, roles and capabilities you need, and where people, AI and other technologies create the most value. We help you design the systems and processes that bring them together, enabling your company to grow without adding complexity at every step.

03◎

Lead through technology change

We upskill teams to navigate technology change. As AI brings technical and non-technical roles closer together, people need both technological understanding and stronger human skills to exercise judgement, make decisions and work effectively together.

We help technical specialists develop a commercial mindset and commercial and operations leaders build the foundational technical understanding to lead the adoption of AI and other technologies.

01—03

The Convergence Point framework

Adoption does not begin with the technology.

It begins by understanding why anyone should change—and what needs to be true for that change to happen.

01

Understand

Who needs to change? What problem are we solving? What value will that change create?

02

De-risk

What needs to be true? Identify the barriers, resources, trust and conditions required.

03

Implement

What can we try? Learn through evidence, adapt the approach, embed what works and scale.
